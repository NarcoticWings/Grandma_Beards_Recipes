Corn Potato Chowder

Makes 8-10 servings.

1 small onion, chopped
1 tablespoon vegetable oil
3 cups chicken broth
2 large potatoes (about 1¼lbs. total), peeled and diced
1½ cups frozen corn kernels
1 can (4½ ounces) mild green chili peppers, drained
Salt to taste, optional
¼ teaspoon black pepper
⅛ teaspoon cayenne pepper
1 cup evaporated milk, half-and-half or milk

In heavy soup pot, saute onion in oil until onion is limp. 
Add chicken broth, diced potatoes, corn, chili pep-pers, salt if desired, black pepper and cayenne. 
Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently until potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
Remove about 2 cups of the solids from the pot and place them in food processor or blender. 
Puree until smooth, adding a little soup liquid if needed to puree the mixture.
Return puree to soup, stirring to combine. (Soup can be made a day ahead to this point, covered and refrigerated.)
Bring soup back to boil. Stir in evaporated milk and heat through.
Taste and add more salt if desired.
