makes thirty 1/2 cup servings

3/4 cup sugar
4 1/2 cups water
1 tbsp instant tea
1 cup lime juice
3 cups orange juice
3 cups grapefruit juice
1 quart ginger ale

combine sugar and 1 1/2 cups water into saucepan over low heat
stir until sugar is dissolved
pour over tea in large bowl
add citrus juices and remaining 3 cups of water
chill
just before serving add ginger ale
pour over orange blossom ice ring or add ice cubes
