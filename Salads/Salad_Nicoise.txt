SALAD NICOISE WITH ARTICHOKES AND ASPARAGUS
(From "Eat Yourself Happy")

12-16 asparagus spears, trimmed
1 large romaine lettuce, coarsely shredded
4 hard-boiled eggs, shelled and quartered
12 artichoke hearts from a jar, halved
1(5-ounce) can tuna in water, drained and flaked
16 black olives, pitted
16 cherry tomatoes, halved
1 large avocado, peeled, pitted and chopped 
crusty whole-wheat bread

Dressing:
1/4 cup red wine vinegar 
1 teaspoon Dijon mustard 
1/2 cup olive oil
2 garlic cloves, crushed 
1 teaspoon dried thyme 
1 teaspoon wild honey 
sea salt and black pepper

To Serve:

Cook the asparagus in a steamer over a saucepan of gently simmering water for 2 minutes, then refresh in cold water. 
Place all the dressing ingredients in a screw-top jar and shake vigorously until well combined. 
Arrange the lettuce in a large bowl or serving plate and place the eggs, artichokes, tuna, olives, tomatoes, avocado, parsley and asparagus on top. 
Drizzle with the dressing and toss lightly until well coated. 
Serve with warm, crusty wholewheat bread.

Salad Nicoise Version 2

For the salad
4 small round potatoes such as Redskins or petit Yukon golds 
1/4 pound of fresh green beans 
4 cups mixed salad greens 
1 can (6 ounces) tuna in oil, drained 
1 medium ripe red tomato, cut into wedges 1/4 cup small black cured olives
2 hard cooked eggs cut into quarters
2 anchovy fillets 

For the Vinaigrette:
2 tablespoons red wine vinegar 
1 teaspoon Dijon mustard 
5 tablespoons olive oil
Kosher salt and cracked black pepper

Place potatoes in a small saucepan of cold, salted water. 
Bring to a boil and cook in boiling water until fork tender. 
Drain, cool and cut into halves or quarters.
In a small sauce pan, bring a pot of cold salted water to a boil. 
Add the beans and cook for 4 minutes. 
Remove beans and place in a bowl of ice water.
Prepare the vinaigrette, place vinegar and mustard in a small boil and whisk together. 
Add in oil in a steady stream while continuing to whisk. 
Season with salt and pepper.
Toss greens with half of the dressing. 
Arrange on a large plate or platter. 
Place tuna in the centre of greens.
Arrange tomatoes, olives, beans, potatoes, and egg wedges around the tuna. 
Place anchovies on top of tuna. 
Drizzle with remaining dressing.
