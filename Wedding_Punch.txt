Makes about 48 3oz servings

1 6oz can frozen lemonade concentrate
6 cups/1 large can of pineapple juice, well chilled
3 bottles champagne, well chilled 
orange slices, maraschino cherries, or strawberries for garnish

mix frozen lemonade concentrate and pineapple juice into punch bowl
add block of ice/1 tray of icecubes
just before serving, add champagne and garnish as desired
