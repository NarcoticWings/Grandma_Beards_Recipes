Makes 20 cup servings, Refills punch bowl twice, and makes 3 cups per guest(all counts based on size of bowl and number of guests)

8 cups/2 quarts cranberry juice
1 can/6oz frozen orange juice
1 can/6oz frozen pineapple juice
1 can/6oz frozen lemon juice(concentrate)
2 cups brandy
2 bottles chilled champagne
Lemon and lime slices

mix cranberry juice, frozen juices, and brandy into large punch bowl over block of ice
just before serving add chilled champagne garnish with lemon and lime slices
