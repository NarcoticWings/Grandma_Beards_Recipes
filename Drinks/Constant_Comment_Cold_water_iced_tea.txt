1. Decide how many quarts of iced tea you want to make
2. Measure into a large saucepan or other container as many quarts of cold fresh water as you need, bring water to a bubbling boil
3. Remove from burner
4. For each quart of boiling water add 4 teabags or 2 rounded tsp of loose tea
5. Let steep for 10 minutes
6. Remove tea bags or strain off lose tea, let cool at room temp. Serve in ice-filled glasses or store in jars for use anytime. 
Lemon and sugar/artificial sweetener may be added anytime.
