2 1/2 cups granulated sugar
1 1/3 cups cocoa (1/4 lb)
1/8 tsp salt
2 cups hot water
1 tsp vanilla

combine first 4 ingredients in order given
stir over medium heat until sugar is dissolved
bring to a boil, boil 2 minutes
cool, add vanilla
May be kept in refrigerator for beverages/flavoring/use as sauce
