Quick Bean and Tuna Salad
Serves 4

1/2 whole grain baguette, torn into 2 inch pieces 
3 tablespoons olive oil 
1 16 ounce can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed 
2 small dill pickles, cut into bite-size pieces 
1 small red onion, thinly sliced 
2 tablespoons red wine vinegar 
1/4 teaspoon pepper 
7 ounce pouch tuna, no salt added, drained and rinsed 
2 tablespoons finally chopped, fresh parsley

Preheat broiler. 
Place the baguette pieces on a heavy cookie shee tablespoon of the oil. 
Place under broiler for about 1 to 2 minute bread pieces and broil for an additional 1 or 2 minutes.
In a large bowl, combine the remaining oil, beans, pickles, onion 
Fold in the broiled baguette pieces. 
Divide the mixture among the tuna and parsley.

*To change up recipe, try tuna seasoned with lemon pepper
