1 tsp allspice
2 2 inch sticks of cinnamon
12 whole cloves
2 quarts cider
1/3 to 2/3 cup brown sugar packed
ground nutmeg

Tie allspice, cinnamon, and cloves into a cheesecloth bag
combine cider and brown sugar into saucepan and heat
add spice bag and simmer 10 minutes or until cider is spiced enough to suit taste
serve piping hot in mugs with dash of nutmeg in each.
