makes thirty-four 1/2 cup servings

3/4 cup sugar
1 1/2 cups water
2 cans(6oz each) frozen concentrated blended grapefruit and orange juice, Reconstituted
2 cans(6oz each) frozen concentrated tangerine juice, Reconstituted
1 quart ginger ale

combine sugar and water in saucepan over low heat, stirring until sugar is dissolved
cool and add chilled juices
just before serving add ginger ale 
pour over orange blossom ice ring or add ice cubes
